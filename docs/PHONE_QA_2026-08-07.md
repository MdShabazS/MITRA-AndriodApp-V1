## Camera Activity Mic Restart Retest

Issue observed:

- While `CameraActivity` was running, the phone played repeated microphone access sounds.
- Earlier logs showed `VOICE_BG` SpeechRecognizer errors/restarts while the camera engine was active.

Root cause:

- `CameraActivity` had started the MITRA background voice runtime during camera QA.
- A pending `BackgroundService.ACTION_RESTART_RUNTIME` alarm could also revive the voice runtime while camera testing continued if `MitraRuntime` was still marked active.

Fix applied:

- `CameraActivity` no longer requests microphone permission automatically.
- `CameraActivity` no longer starts the background voice service automatically.
- On camera QA entry, `CameraActivity` clears `MitraRuntime` active state and sends `ACTION_STOP_MITRA_RUNTIME` so pending voice runtime restarts are cancelled.

Validation performed:

- Rebuilt debug APK successfully with `:app:assembleDebug`.
- Installed APK on the OPPO test phone with `adb install -r -d -g`.
- Force-stopped the app, cleared logcat, and launched `com.unique.visionmate/.CameraActivity`.
- Confirmed camera inference stayed active with continuous `CameraTest` and `HAZARD` logs through frame 25+.
- Confirmed no `VOICE_BG` logs during the patched camera run.
- Confirmed no `ACTION_RESTART_RUNTIME` intent appeared during the patched camera run.
- Confirmed the only background service intent was `ACTION_STOP_MITRA_RUNTIME` from `CameraActivity`.

Result:

- PASS for camera QA path: camera inference continues and the mic/STT restart loop does not reappear.
